About him

Bashir is my older brother. When people ask me who I admire most, I don't even have to think about it. It's him.

He's the kind of person who is quiet most of the time, but when you need him he's always there. He has been looking out for me since I was small, and honestly I don't think he even realizes how much it meant to me.

What I admire about him

He works hard

I've seen him come home tired and still sit down to finish what he had to do. He doesn't complain about it either. He just gets it done.

He's kind to people

It doesn't matter if it's a friend, a neighbour or someone he just met. He's polite to everyone and helps if he can.

He's patient with me

I ask a lot of questions and make a lot of mistakes. He never gets annoyed. He just shows me again until I get it.

A story I remember

One time I did really badly on a test and I hid the paper in my bag because I was scared of what everyone would say. Bashir saw I was acting weird and asked me what was wrong. I showed him, and he didn't get angry at all. He just said "okay, let's fix it." We sat together almost every evening that week going over the questions I got wrong. The next test I did much better, and he was happier about it than I was.

"Just do your best and be honest. The rest will sort itself out."
